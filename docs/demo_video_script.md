# Demo video script (about 2.5 minutes)

**How to record:** Windows key + Alt + R starts and stops the Xbox Game Bar recording, or use the free OBS. Record the dashboard window with your voice. Upload it to YouTube as **Unlisted** (or Google Drive with "anyone with the link") and paste the link into the form.

Before recording:
- Start the dashboard: `python -m streamlit run app/streamlit_app.py`, or open the public URL.
- Select **Kamrup → Field crops**.
- Zoom the browser to 90 %.

| Time | Screen | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Sign-in page | "This is PotharSight Assam, our solution for NESFIC challenge D13. Pothar means field in Assamese. An agriculture or district official signs in with their user ID." |
| 0:15–0:35 | Step 1: district cards | "Today, the official figure for unused cultivable land exists only as a number in a table. PotharSight shows exactly where that land is. The official picks a district – here Kamrup Rural, one of our four districts." |
| 0:35–0:50 | Step 2 → Field crops; Step 3 → keep all three land types, block = All, 1 hectare | "Then they say what they want to grow, and what kind of land they want: empty land, farmland unused for years, or fields left empty in winter where a second crop is possible." |
| 0:50–1:15 | Results: numbers + map; click a dot | "PotharSight finds over two thousand suitable sites in Kamrup. Each dot is a site. Clicking it tells the official, in plain words, why it is suitable and what to watch out for – for example acidic soil that needs liming." |
| 1:15–1:35 | Best sites list → "Open in Google Maps" | "The best sites are listed first, each with a link that opens it in Google Maps, so field staff can go straight there." |
| 1:35–1:50 | Compare blocks | "Blocks are compared, so programme targets can go where the land actually is: Boko, Rampur and Goroimari come first." |
| 1:50–2:00 | Download buttons | "The list downloads as Excel, as a Google Earth file, or for any GIS." |
| 2:00–2:15 | New search → Cachar → Fruits & vegetables (then show Lakhimpur / Dibrugarh cards) | "The same works for fruits and vegetables, which need safer land from floods, and for every district: Cachar in the Barak valley, and Lakhimpur and Dibrugarh, which we added with one configuration line each." |
| 2:15–2:40 | Advanced analysis page: suitability map, weight sliders, validation | "For ASSAC's GIS staff there is an advanced page: suitability classes, adjustable criteria, factor scores, validation against official statistics and downloadable GeoTIFF and GeoPackage layers. Everything is built from free satellite and open data." |
| 2:40–2:50 | Back to the sign-in page or the map | "PotharSight supports decisions; ownership is always confirmed by the Revenue Department before any allotment. Thank you." |
